# Module · Resident portal (Release 2)

**Purpose** The home's digital front door.
**Users** residents (owners/tenants), Sigve/operator, support.

## Features
- My home: documents (contracts, manuals, warranties), systems installed, maintenance calendar
- My energy: consumption/production/SOC, comparison with modelled expectation and with the field average
  (anonymised), tips
- Support tickets; notices from the operator; community board; shared bookings (if any shared assets)
- Consents: what data is shared with the field, research, optimisation
- BankID login

## Data
`unit`, `meter`, `reading`, `document`, consents.

## 3D
"My house" viewpoint in the twin with own data lit up.

## Acceptance
- A resident sees only their own meters; field views are aggregated
- Export/delete own data works (GDPR)
