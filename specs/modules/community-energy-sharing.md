# Module · Community energy sharing (Release 3)

**Purpose** Share locally produced energy fairly and transparently across the field.
**Users** residents, operator, energy team; regulator context (Norwegian rules for *deling av
egenprodusert strøm* / local energy communities — to be confirmed by the energy group).

## Features
- Allocation rules (equal / by demand / by stake), simulated before enabling
- Settlement statements per home per month; explanation of each kWh's origin
- Fairness dashboard; opt-in/opt-out; dispute log
- Integration with the hub controller and metering

## 3D
The flows in the living field are this module's data made visible.

## Acceptance
- Statements reconcile with meter totals to < 0.5 %
- Rules changes are versioned and visible to all residents
