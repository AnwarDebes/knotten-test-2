# 11 · Brand and content

## Identity (from the existing logo)
- Name: **KNOTTEN** — subline *Sjøutsikt i Rødberg* — locators *Snigsfjorden · Lindesnes · ved Audna elvas utløp*
- Mark: arch/circle vignette with cabins, pines and the fjord; serif wordmark; owner line *Sigve Simonsen AS*
- Palette (derive exact values from the logo file): deep pine green, fjord blue-grey, warm timber/ochre,
  bone white, night navy for the twin; accent amber for energy flow
- Typography: high-contrast serif for headlines (matching the wordmark), humanist sans for UI and data
- Motion: slow, weather-like; nothing bounces. Energy flow is the only fast motion.

## Tone
Quiet confidence. Norwegian first, plain. Numbers with sources. Winter light is beautiful — say so.
Avoid: "unique", "stunning", "luxury". Prefer: measured, specific, local names (Audna, Snigsfjorden,
Rødberg, Raudberg, Fløyen).
